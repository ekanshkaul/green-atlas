FIELD SAFETY REFERENCE

Important safety notice

This page is a project reference, not a clinically approved protocol. Classifications and first-aid notes are shown as supplied in the flora dataset; source citations and clinical review status are not recorded. A “Safe” label is a dataset label only, not a guarantee of harmlessness.

For suspected poisoning, ingestion, eye exposure, breathing difficulty, collapse or severe symptoms, seek urgent professional medical care.

General Field Exposure Reminders

These are cautious reminders, not an approved treatment algorithm. Follow campus medical guidance and instructions from emergency services or qualified medical personnel. Specific plant-related symptoms and treatment should be confirmed against reliable clinical sources.

Dataset Label Prudent Field Response Precaution
Toxic Stop handling and prevent further exposure. For ingestion, eye exposure or symptoms, seek urgent medical help and follow professional instructions. Avoid ingestion and direct contact. Use task-appropriate gloves and eye protection.
Mild Irritant Stop contact if irritation occurs. Follow the approved campus response procedure and seek medical advice if symptoms persist or worsen. Use gloves and avoid touching the eyes or face during sampling.
Listed Non-Toxic The label is only the value stored in this dataset; it does not rule out allergies, irritants or other risks. Use standard field hygiene and avoid eating unidentified plant material.
