Data scope and limitations

This page summarizes the supplied flora dataset and the project survey schedule. The dataset does not include quadrat abundance counts, tree diameters, biomass equations, measured carbon-stock values, or surveyed sector areas, so carbon-sequestration, Shannon–Wiener diversity and invasive-pressure figures are not presented as results.

Invasive behaviour is mentioned in some field notes; this is a text flag, not a measured pressure index.

Survey statuses reflect the project schedule. Completion records and field sheets are not included in the supplied dataset.

Ecological Sampling Methodology

The catalogue records presence data: botanical and common names, family, habit, sector assignment, coordinates where supplied, elevation tags where supplied, toxicity notes and a per-record taxon verification flag. It is not an abundance survey, and these records cannot support a Shannon–Wiener diversity index or a carbon-stock estimate on their own.

The mapped records carry supplied elevation tags spanning not recorded; this is the range of those tags, not an independently surveyed elevation gradient. Sector polygons and areas shown on the Maps page are working approximations derived from supplied sector extents, not surveyed boundaries.
